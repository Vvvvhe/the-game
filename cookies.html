Cookie policy

Effective 8 October 2026

This website does not use cookies. It also does not use local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, tracking pixels, device fingerprinting or any other technology that stores or reads information on your device.

Why there is no cookie banner

Laws such as the EU ePrivacy Directive and the UK PECR require consent before non-essential information is stored on, or read from, your device. Because this website stores and reads nothing, there is nothing to consent to, so we do not show a cookie banner.

Third parties

No third-party content is embedded: no analytics, advertising, social-media buttons, videos, maps or externally hosted fonts. Third parties therefore cannot set cookies through this website.

The game

The desktop game saves your progress and settings in its own data folder on your computer. This is part of the game you chose to install. It is not a website cookie, and it is never sent to us. See the privacy policy for where it is stored and how to remove it.

Changes

If we ever add cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy first. Where they are not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent before setting them.
