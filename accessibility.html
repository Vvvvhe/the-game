Accessibility

Last reviewed 8 October 2026

This website

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Specifically:

  • every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to main content” link;
  • text and controls meet the WCAG AA contrast ratio (at least 4.5:1 for normal text);
  • every informative image has a text description;
  • form fields have visible labels, required fields are announced, and errors are explained in text and moved into focus;
  • pages use headings, landmarks and lists so screen readers can navigate them;
  • nothing moves or plays automatically, and smooth scrolling is turned off if your system asks for reduced motion;
  • text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content.

These points were checked with automated testing and keyboard testing on 8 October 2026. The site has not yet been tested by people who use assistive technology every day.

The game

The game has limitations that we are open about:

  • it needs a keyboard and mouse, and controls cannot yet be remapped;
  • it is not usable with a screen reader;
  • story dialogue is shown as on-screen text as well as spoken;
  • explosions and neon effects include flashing light (see the health notice in the terms);
  • the Low graphics setting in the pause menu reduces visual effects and is easier to run on slower computers.

Feedback

If something does not work for you, email theacreatorstudios@gmail.com or use the contact form. Tell us the page or feature and the assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 10 working days.
