Refund policy

Effective 8 October 2026

The Game is free. We do not take payments on this website or in the game. There are no in-game purchases, subscriptions or donations. Because you are never charged, there is nothing to refund.

If someone charged you for this game

We do not sell the game anywhere. If a website, store or person charged you for it, they are not authorised by us. Ask them or your payment provider for your money back, and please tell us at theacreatorstudios@gmail.com so we can act.

If we start charging in the future

Before any paid version or purchase is offered, we will publish a full refund policy here. It will include your statutory rights. For example, in the European Union and the United Kingdom consumers have a 14-day right to withdraw from digital purchases, unless they expressly agree that download starts immediately and acknowledge they lose that right. Purchases made before a change keep the policy in force when they were made.
