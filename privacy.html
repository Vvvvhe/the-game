Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website, download the game, play it, or contact us. In short: the website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising, the game collects nothing, and we receive personal data only if you email us.

This policy is written to meet the Law of the Republic of Armenia on Protection of Personal Data and, for visitors in the European Union and United Kingdom, the GDPR and UK GDPR.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Acreator Studio (Vahe Hakobyan), Yerevan (full postal address available on request by email), Armenia. Email: theacreatorstudios@gmail.com. Full details are in the legal notice.

Visiting this website

Like any website, this one is delivered by a hosting provider: GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (United States). Downloads are delivered by GitHub Releases, operated by GitHub, Inc. (United States). To deliver pages and files and to protect against abuse, these providers process technical data automatically: your IP address, the date and time, the page or file requested, your browser’s user-agent string and the amount of data sent. The providers keep these server logs for as long as GitHub needs them for security purposes (GitHub does not publish a fixed period for these logs; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement). We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors.

Legal basis (where the GDPR or similar laws apply): our legitimate interest in delivering the website securely (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

This website does not:

  • set cookies or use local storage, session storage or similar technologies (see the cookie policy);
  • use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or social-media plugins;
  • embed videos, maps, fonts or other content from third-party servers. Fonts and images are served from this website.

The download page checks your operating system inside your browser to highlight the matching file. This check runs only on your device; the result is not stored or sent anywhere.

Playing the game

The game does not connect to the internet. It has no accounts, telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or in-game purchases. Your progress, settings and the five-digit save code you choose are stored only on your own computer. We cannot see them, and we cannot recover them for you. To delete them, remove the game’s data folder. It keeps the game’s earlier name, Sunspot County, so saves carry over from older versions:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Sunspot County
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Sunspot County
  • Linux: ~/.config/Sunspot County

Contacting us

If you email us, directly or by using the contact form to prepare an email, we receive your email address, your message, any name you give, and the usual email metadata. We use this only to read and answer your message, and to keep a record of privacy or legal requests. We keep it for 12 months after our last reply unless the law requires us to keep it longer, for example for a legal claim.

Legal basis: your consent, which you give with the checkbox on the form or by choosing to email us (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in answering messages (Article 6(1)(f)). You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your messages.

Our email provider, Google (Gmail), stores these emails on our behalf and may process them in other countries under its own privacy terms.

Who receives data

We do not sell or share personal data. The only other parties that process it are the hosting providers named above and our email provider, Google (Gmail), which process it on our behalf. Some of these providers may store data outside your country. Where the law requires it, they rely on legal safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

This website and the game are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete the message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email theacreatorstudios@gmail.com. We will answer within the time the law requires, normally within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Armenia, the Personal Data Protection Agency of the Ministry of Justice; in the EU or UK, the authority in your country.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS and loads no third-party scripts. Download files come with SHA-256 checksums so you can check that they have not been altered.

Changes

If we change how data is handled, for example by adding analytics, we will update this page and its date first. Where the law requires consent, we will ask for it before any new processing starts.
