Game content

The Game is developed by Acreator Studio (Vahe Hakobyan). Its characters, 3D models, world layouts, textures, story and interface are original works created for the game. The textures and the snake model are generated by the project’s own scripts. All screenshots on this website are captured from the game.

Dialogue voice recordings: Voices generated with Runway (runway.com). Under Runway’s Terms of Use (section 4.4), Runway does not claim ownership of generated output and does not restrict its commercial use. The voices are preset AI voices, not imitations of real people.

Sound effects (engine, tyre skid, crashes, explosions, building collapse, gunshots, impacts and pain reactions): Original effects generated with Runway (runway.com), under the same Runway Terms of Use (section 4.4).

The public downloads contain no licensed commercial music. The in-car radio plays a synthesized station generated by the game. The game’s sound effects are included.
